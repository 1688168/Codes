# OpenAI / Anthropic SDE Labor Market Research

# High-Level Summary

OpenAI and Anthropic are NOT only hiring:
```text
ML PhDs or pure AI researchers
```

They heavily hire:
- distributed systems engineers
- infrastructure engineers
- backend engineers
- data platform engineers
- applied AI engineers
- performance/inference engineers
- reliability/safety engineers

A strong Amazon SDE II background is highly relevant.

Especially:
```text
large-scale distributed systems
low latency systems
high throughput systems
data-heavy production infrastructure
```
